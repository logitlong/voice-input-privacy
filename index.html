Privacy Policy

Voice Input for Claude, ChatGPT, Doubao, DeepSeek & Qianwen (Chrome extension)
Last updated: November 11, 2026

Summary

This extension does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What the extension does with your audio

When you start dictation, the extension passes audio from your microphone, and from your computer's audio if you choose that source, to Chrome's built-in speech recognition (the Web Speech API). Chrome sends that audio to Google's speech recognition service to convert it to text. That processing is done by Google and is governed by Google's privacy policy. The extension itself never records, saves or transmits your audio anywhere else.

Audio is only captured while dictation is on, which is shown by the highlighted button and the badge on the extension icon. To listen to computer audio, Chrome asks you to share a screen or tab; the extension uses only the audio track and does not read, record or transmit the picture.

What the extension does with the recognized text

The recognized text is inserted into the message box of the supported AI chat page you are on (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, doubao.com, chat.deepseek.com, qianwen.com and workbuddy.cn). If you use the "dictate and send" button, the extension also presses that page's send button for you. The extension does not keep a copy of the text and does not read your conversations.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (recognition language, recently used languages, audio source and interface language) are saved locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage. They never leave your device and are deleted when you remove the extension.

Permissions

storage: to remember the settings listed above.
Access to the supported sites (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, doubao.com, chat.deepseek.com, qianwen.com and workbuddy.cn): to show the dictation toolbar next to the message box and to insert the recognized text into it.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Google, through Chrome's built-in speech recognition as described above. The extension is an independent project and is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, ByteDance, DeepSeek, Alibaba or Tencent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

概要

本扩展不收集、不保存、不出售、不分享你的个人数据。它没有服务器，没有账号，没有统计分析，也没有广告。

扩展如何处理你的声音

开始识别后，扩展会把麦克风的声音（如果你选择了电脑声音，也包括电脑里播放的声音）交给 Chrome 内置的语音识别（Web Speech API）。Chrome 会把这些音频发送到 Google 的语音识别服务转换成文字，这一处理由 Google 完成，适用 Google 的隐私政策。扩展本身不会录制、保存音频，也不会把音频发送到其他任何地方。

只有在识别开启期间才会采集声音，此时按钮会高亮，扩展图标上会显示标记。识别电脑声音时，Chrome 会要求你共享屏幕或标签页；扩展只使用其中的音频，不读取、不录制、不传输画面。

扩展如何处理识别出的文字

识别出的文字会写入当前所在的受支持 AI 对话页面（claude.ai、chatgpt.com、doubao.com、chat.deepseek.com、qianwen.com、workbuddy.cn）的输入框。如果你使用“转录并发送”按钮，扩展还会替你点击页面的发送按钮。扩展不会保留文字副本，也不会读取你的对话内容。

保存在你设备上的数据

你的设置（识别语言、最近使用的语言、声音来源、界面语言）通过 Chrome 的扩展存储保存在本地浏览器中，不会离开你的设备，卸载扩展时会一并删除。

权限

storage：用于记住上述设置。
访问受支持的网站（claude.ai、chatgpt.com、doubao.com、chat.deepseek.com、qianwen.com、workbuddy.cn）：用于在输入框旁显示工具条，并把识别出的文字写入输入框。
